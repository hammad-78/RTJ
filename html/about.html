About Road To Jannat Welfare Foundation

Serving the ummah with sincerity, transparency, and excellence.

Our Story

Road To Jannat Welfare Foundation began as a small community initiative, when a group of friends were moved by stories of families sleeping hungry and children walking miles for water. What started as a simple food distribution drive quickly grew into a structured, transparent charity effort.

Today, by Allah’s permission, we support projects across multiple countries, partnering with trustworthy local organizations that understand the real, on-the-ground needs of their communities. Our volunteers and donors form a growing family, united by the intention to seek Allah’s pleasure through meaningful service.

Our Mission

To connect the generosity of believers to impactful, Shariah-compliant projects that protect dignity, relieve hardship, and open long-term opportunities for those in need.

Our Vision

A world where every believer’s charity becomes a continuous source of benefit – a road paved with mercy, leading to Jannah, in sha Allah.

Our Core Values

Ikhlas (Sincerity)

Every campaign, decision, and communication is rooted in the intention of seeking Allah’s pleasure alone.

Amanah (Trust)

We treat every donation as a trust that must be handled with utmost care, accuracy, and accountability.

Ihsan (Excellence)

We strive to plan thoroughly, execute professionally, and communicate clearly – going the extra mile for beneficiaries and donors alike.

Rahmah (Mercy)

We prioritize dignity and compassion, recognizing that receiving help should never come with humiliation.

How We Work

  • Identify genuine needs with verified local partners and community leaders.
  • Design projects with clear goals, budgets, and sustainability in mind.
  • Allocate funds according to Shariah guidelines and donor intentions.
  • Monitor implementation and collect updates, photos, and testimonials.
  • Share transparent reports with donors and the wider community.

Shariah & Compliance

Our advisory board includes students of knowledge and professionals who review our zakat policies, project selection, and internal controls. While we are constantly improving, we are committed to aligning our operations with Islamic ethics and best-in-class non-profit standards.

We also work with auditors and legal advisors to ensure compliance with local regulations in the countries where we operate.
